Two years getting AI systems live with enterprise customers.

Most of that work sits between a demo that works and a customer who relies on it. Test cases written from how the customer really works, graded by plain rules and a judge, and run more than once so a lucky pass does not count.

A bar for go, partial or no-go that everyone agrees on before launch, so the rollout call is a quick check. And before anything ships, a look at the customer's own data and setup, unit by unit, with each fix sent to the team that owns it.

The builds on this site are that work, rebuilt on synthetic or public data so they can be shown in the open. Most have a demo page that needs no install.
